02 — Overview

One procurement platform. Every workflow built in.

ProQscan AI is an enterprise procurement management and forensic audit platform built to oversee capital expenditure across purchasing, warehouse storage, audit verification, and executive intelligence. Rather than forcing organizations to bridge disparate third-party tools, the product establishes an unbroken operational trail where every purchase order, goods receipt note (GRN), and vendor invoice is reconciled against the same live data layer.

03 — The Challenge

Procurement should not live in disconnected systems.

In traditional enterprise environments, departments operate in disconnected silos. Purchasing creates purchase orders in an isolated ERP; warehouse receiving logs physical shipments on paper or localized spreadsheets; and accounting pays invoices blindly without verifying what physically crossed the loading dock. By the time audits happen months later, fraudulent overbilling, duplicate invoices, and unauthorized vendor price inflation are already paid out.

✕ Fragmented Enterprise Baseline

Siloed Tools & Operational Gaps

Information gaps between departments hide discrepancies until post-audits occur.

  • ✕
    Isolated Requisitions: Purchase Orders created without visibility into current warehouse balances.
  • ✕
    Unverified Receiving: Warehouse clerks log goods on paper forms, leaving quantity and quality discrepancies disconnected from finance.
  • ✕
    Blind Invoice Settlement: Accounts payable settles vendor claims without proof of actual item receipt.
  • ✕
    Retroactive Auditing: Anomaly detection happens after funds have already departed company accounts.
✓ Connected ProQscan Architecture

Continuous 3-Way Reconciliation

A unified data layer reconciling requisitions, warehouse goods receipts, and invoices in real time.

04 — Users & Operational Roles

Designed for distinct operators with contrasting cognitive needs.

A major UX hurdle was preventing the interface from becoming an overwhelming generic spreadsheet. I structured dedicated workspaces tuned specifically to each operator's tasks:

ROLE 01

Procurement Officer

Needs to generate POs, parse Comparative Statements (CS) across multiple vendor quotes, and spot unit price inflation before approvals.

ROLE 02

Warehouse Receiving Officer

Operates in noisy warehouse environments. Needs rapid keyboard/barcode logging, star-rated quality notes, and instant GRN generation.

ROLE 03

Forensic Compliance Auditor

Investigates flagged transactions. Needs drill-down evidence drawers, Benford's Law digit tests, and immutable audit disposition tracking.

ROLE 04

Executive Leadership (C-Suite)

Monitors high-level capital exposure. Needs aggregate spend vs. risk curves, vendor concentration metrics, and instant departmental drill-downs.

05 — Product Workflow

From Requisition to Forensic Clearance: The 4-Stage Operational Trail

The system maps the end-to-end operational lifecycle so every transaction leaves a clear, tamper-proof paper trail:

STAGE 01 — ENTRY

Requisition & Sourcing

Department files item request. System checks current warehouse stock to prevent duplicate orders before launching vendor quote comparisons.

STAGE 02 — ACTION

PO Generation & Delivery

Lowest compliant vendor selected via Comparative Statement. PO is dispatched; active tracking monitors lead times and transit status.

STAGE 03 — DECISION

Dock Receiving & 3-Way Check

Physical goods arrive. Warehouse records received counts, damage notes, and bill numbers. System verifies PO vs. GRN line by line.

STAGE 04 — COMPLETION

Forensic Scan & Settlement

Invoice passes through 9 automated risk vector checks and Benford's Law distribution test. Cleared bills move to finance; anomalies halt for review.

06 — Platform Architecture

Four specialized engines. One unified operational spine.

Each portal provides focused functionality while sharing real-time relational state across the database:

ENGINE 01 PROCUREMENT PORTAL

Procurement & Vendor Sourcing

Dashboard Generate PO Sourcing CS Entry Vendor Evaluation Active Procurements
  • Guided 3-Step PO Builder: Supports file drag-and-drop parsing as well as step-by-step entry: PO Info → Requisition Details → Item Specs.
  • Comparative Statement (CS) Sourcing: Direct matrix entry of vendor quotes with automatic lowest-price highlight and price inflation alerts against historical records.
  • Vendor Behavior History: Compares supplier candidates on historical fulfillment rates, delivery speed, and past quality feedback.
  • Active Procurement Pipeline: Live status indicators (In Transit, Awaiting Dispatch, Overdue) with direct drill-down into delivery logs.
Key UX Innovation: Flags vendor price inflation against historical quotes before the purchase order is finalized or approved.
ENGINE 02 INVENTORY PORTAL

Stock Receiving & Material Issuance

Dashboard Stock IN (Receiving) Stock OUT (Issuance) Stock Balances Transaction Ledger
  • Warehouse Control KPIs: Real-time tracking of Total Inventory Value, Low Stock Alerts, Dormant Stock (6+ months unused), and Rejection Rates.
  • Stock IN "Log Delivery" Modal: Enables receiving clerks to capture GRN details, bill numbers, verified counts, and star-rated product quality ratings before PO acceptance.
  • Material Issue Register: Searchable disbursement log tracking item IDs, quantities, recipient names, and requesting department codes.
  • Historical Transaction Ledger: Expandable record per addition/issuance with direct PO/GRN cross-references.
Key UX Innovation: Pre-submission inventory check prevents redundant procurement requests if active warehouse stock already exists.
ENGINE 03 FORENSIC AUDIT ENGINE

Anomaly Scanner & Benford's Law Audit

Audit Dashboard Anomaly Scanner Benford's Law Audit Outliers Analysis
  • 9-Vector Anomaly Scanner: Filters flagged items across 9 risk categories: Price Inflation, Overbilled, Under-Delivered, Pending Orders, Unbilled Deliveries, Excess GRN, Supplier Favoritism, Missing CS, and Duplicate Billing.
  • Benford's Law Statistical Engine: Chi-squared statistical algorithm evaluating invoice leading-digit distribution to detect artificial billing numbers, complete with plain-language explainers and "Manipulation Detected" alert banners.
  • Structured 4-Stage Case Disposition: Structured workflow: Detected → Check Required → Action Taken → Cleared with immutable investigator notes.
  • Evidence Drilldown Drawer: Opens Comparative Statement baseline, delivery logs, vendor history, and product quality stars without leaving the main audit queue.
Key UX Innovation: Translates complex statistical chi-square tests into intuitive digit-by-digit visual verdicts (Suspicious vs. Normal).
ENGINE 04 EXECUTIVE ENGINE

Executive Intelligence & Oversight

Executive Summary Spend Analytics Vendor Scorecards Delivery Health Department Drilldown
  • Executive KPI Matrix: High-contrast summary tiles displaying Total Spend (৳ Taka), Tickets at Risk, Active POs in Pipeline, Pending Deliveries, and Overdue Value.
  • Spend vs. Risk Exposure Curve: Dual-curve chart exposing the direct relationship between procurement volume spikes and detected risk spikes.
  • Vendor Performance Matrix: Top-tier vendor spend concentration, newly flagged suppliers, and complete scorecard distribution.
  • Departmental Lens Drilldown: Allows leadership to inspect operations through a specific officer's viewpoint (e.g., "Viewing Procurement Officer: Rahul Ahmed").
Key UX Innovation: Instant context-switching from corporate-level rollups directly down into granular departmental evidence.
07 — Key UX Decisions

Designing for high data density without cognitive fatigue.

Enterprise tools often fail because they dump uncurated spreadsheets onto users. I structured ProQscan AI around progressive disclosure, unambiguous risk semantics, and zero-confusion reconciliation:

COLOR SEMANTICS

Strict Forensic Risk Grammar

Rather than decorative colors, every hue in ProQscan AI has an unambiguous operational meaning:
• Emerald Green: Verified 3-way match, healthy stock, on-time delivery.
• Amber Orange: Pending approval, unbilled deliveries awaiting verification.
• Crimson Red: Critical anomalies, Benford manipulation alerts, duplicate billing.
• Navy Blue: Strategic governance metrics and executive intelligence indicators.

PROGRESSIVE DISCLOSURE

Contextual Sliders & Evidence Drawers

Instead of navigating away to inspect flagged transactions, auditors click any anomaly row to open a slide-over evidence drawer. This reveals the original PO, supplier quotation, and dock GRN without losing the auditor's scroll position in the primary queue.

INPUT ERGONOMICS

Keyboard-First Dock Receiving

Warehouse clerks logging incoming deliveries need speed. I optimized the "Log Delivery" form for sequential keyboard navigation (Tab-index ordering, numeric auto-formatting, and instant barcode scan population) to minimize mouse dependency.

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE

Single Unbroken Audit Trail

Every record maintains explicit bidirectional links: a purchase order displays its related GRN and invoice; a flagged invoice links directly to its receiving condition stars and the originating requisition officer.

08 — Key Screen Showcases

Deep dive into core operational screens.

Forensic Engine

Forensic Anomaly Scanner & Benford's Law Audit

What it is

An automated audit workspace that runs multi-vector risk tests on all incoming invoice data.

What the user does

Auditors review flagged invoices, inspect leading-digit distribution graphs, and log official case disposition notes.

Why structured this way

Translates complex mathematical chi-square tests into clear visual verdicts (Suspicious vs. Normal), enabling non-statisticians to act quickly.

Inventory Engine

Stock Receiving & Material Disbursement Ledger

What it is

The warehouse intake and issuance hub tracking items from loading dock to internal departmental use.

What the user does

Warehouse clerks log incoming GRNs against open POs, rate item quality, and record authorized stock disbursements.

Why structured this way

Prevents unrecorded inventory leakage by enforcing mandatory bill numbers and condition star ratings before goods are added to active balances.

Executive Engine

Executive Summary & Spend vs. Risk Analysis

What it is

The C-suite overview aggregating capital flow, active order pipelines, and enterprise risk tickets.

What the user does

Executives monitor total corporate spend in real-time Taka (৳), track risk trends, and drill down into specific departmental operations.

Why structured this way

Pairs high-contrast aggregate KPI cards with a visual correlation curve, instantly revealing if a spending surge coincides with an anomaly surge.

Procurement Engine

PO Generation & Comparative Statement Sourcing

What it is

The purchasing command center where requisitions are transformed into verified vendor purchase orders.

What the user does

Procurement officers compare supplier quotations, identify lowest compliant bids, and dispatch structured POs.

Why structured this way

Automates quote comparison arithmetic and alerts officers to price inflation against historical purchase rates before commitments are approved.

09 — Design System & UI System

A consistent design system built for multi-portal scale.

To maintain visual coherence across all four engines, I established a centralized design tokens system:

TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Manrope + Tabular Monospace

Headings & UI: Clean geometric sans-serif (Manrope / Plus Jakarta Sans) for effortless scanning of dense dashboards.
Financial Numerals: Tabular monospace figures for invoice amounts, PO numbers, and ledger columns to ensure vertical decimal alignment across multi-row comparison tables.

COMPONENT TOKENS

Atomic Table & Status Badges

Standardized pill badges (Verified, Pending, Flagged, Cleared), high-density data table rows with hover elevation, sticky column headers, and uniform pagination controls shared across all four operational portals.
