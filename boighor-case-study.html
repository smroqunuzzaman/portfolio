02 — Overview

A pocket library engineered for long-form reading comfort.

BoiGhor is a dedicated mobile reading and audiobook listening application developed to bridge literary collections with everyday mobile users. The app addresses three primary reader needs: comfortable digital text rendering for hours of reading without eye strain, intuitive audio playback for busy commuters listening on the go, and digital library shelf management with automated return notifications.

03 — The Challenge

Digital reading on mobile often feels commercialized, cramped, and fatiguing.

Many existing digital reading apps suffer from aggressive storefront promotions, rigid typographic layouts, and clumsy audio scrubbers that are nearly impossible to adjust while walking or standing on public transit. Readers frequently face eye fatigue in low light, lose their reading place when transitioning between audio and text, and forget book loan due dates.

✕ Conventional E-Reader Apps

Commercial Distractions & Clumsy Controls

Tiny progress sliders, intrusive purchase popups, and fixed margins that cause visual strain.

  • ✕
    Rigid Typography: Inflexible margins and line-heights that crowd text on smaller mobile screens.
  • ✕
    Tiny Audio Sliders: Narrow scrub bars that make precise 15-second rewinds difficult during transit.
  • ✕
    Disconnected Media: Reading an e-book and listening to its audiobook are treated as completely separate apps with independent bookmarks.
✓ BoiGhor Purpose-Built App

Ergonomic Canvas & Synchronized Media

An invisible, typography-first reading canvas with thumb-friendly audio controls.

  • ✓
    Adaptive Reading Canvas: Custom font scales, line leading, and eye-friendly Sepia / Midnight color themes.
  • ✓
    Thumb-Zone Audio Sheet: Expandable bottom player with tactile 15-second skip buttons and sleep timer.
  • ✓
    Unified Progress Sync: Single shelf bookmarking that tracks your spot whether reading paragraphs or listening to narration.
04 — Users & Mobile Personas

Designing for diverse reading habits throughout the day.

Mobile reading behavior shifts dramatically depending on environment and available attention:

PERSONA 01

The Daily Commuter

Listens to narrated audiobooks while navigating crowded buses and trains. Needs large, unmissable play/pause targets and offline storage.

PERSONA 02

The Bedtime Reader

Reads long-form literature in dark bedrooms. Needs warm sepia tones, zero blue-light glare, and an automatic audio sleep timer.

PERSONA 03

The Student / Researcher

Highlights key passages, exports chapter summaries, and references digital library books with automated due-date reminders.

PERSONA 04

The Casual Explorer

Discovers emerging Bengali and world literature through curated editorial lists, sample audio clips, and community reviews.

05 — Product Workflow

The Reading Lifecycle: From Shelf Discovery to Offline Immersion

How users discover, read, listen, and annotate books in BoiGhor:

STEP 01 — ENTRY

Library Discovery

Browse curated shelves, listen to narrator audio previews, and reserve or download titles directly to personal shelf.

STEP 02 — ACTION

Immersive Reading Canvas

Tap book to open full-bleed reader. Adjust font size, margin width, and background paper texture with real-time preview.

STEP 03 — DECISION

Seamless Audio Handoff

Switching to hands-free? Tap the listen icon; audio narrator begins speaking from the exact paragraph you were reading.

STEP 04 — COMPLETION

Retention & Return

Review highlights and reading milestones. Automated notification alerts user before library loan expires.

06 — UX Structure

Core architectural modules of the BoiGhor mobile app.

The interface balances quiet literary immersion with fluid audio multitasking:

Font Scale Sepia / Dark Page Curl Highlighting

  • Custom typography menu supporting font switching, leading adjustments, and margin padding sliders.

MODULE 02 AUDIO STUDIO

Ergonomic Audiobook Player

Thumb Zone 15s Jump Sleep Timer Speed 0.75x–2x
  • Expandable bottom-sheet audio player accessible from anywhere in the app with a quick upward swipe.
  • Generously sized 15-second forward/backward jump buttons designed for one-handed operation on transit.
  • Gentle fade-out sleep timer (15 min, 30 min, end of chapter) preventing audio from running all night.
07 — Key UX Decisions

Designing for invisible ergonomics and literary respect.

Every detail was refined to remove friction between the reader and the written word:

ERGONOMICS

Thumb-Zone Audio Controls

Reaching for the top of large smartphones while standing on a moving train causes dropped phones and mis-taps. All primary playback buttons (Play/Pause, 15s skip, scrubber) sit comfortably in the bottom 40% of the screen.

TYPOGRAPHY UX

Optically Calibrated Leading

Bengali script and classical typography require greater line height than English sans-serif to prevent ascenders and descenders from colliding. I engineered proportional leading scaling that expands automatically as font size increases.

OFFLINE RELIABILITY

Transparent Storage & Sync

Readers frequently travel through underground metros or rural areas with zero signal. Downloaded titles display clear offline badges, and all progress is cached locally and merged quietly whenever a connection is restored.

CULTURAL IDENTITY

Bengali Warmth without Cliché

Rather than relying on generic modern neon gradients, the brand identity embraces warm bookcloth textures, terracotta tones, and deep midnight blues reflecting the rich heritage of South Asian literature.

09 — Design System

Paper-inspired tactile design tokens for mobile screens.

The design system pairs tactile warmth with high legibility across diverse device screens:

COLOR SYSTEM

Paper Tones: Sepia, Parchment & Midnight

Carefully calibrated background values (`#F7F4EB` Day, `#EFE6D5` Sepia, `#121214` Midnight) provide optimal contrast ratios that exceed WCAG AAA standards while preventing glare.

TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Editorial Serif + Modern Sans

Playfair Display and classical serif typefaces give book covers and titles literary gravitas, while Plus Jakarta Sans powers library navigation, search results, and player controls with crisp clarity.

11 — Designer Reflection

What this project taught me as a product designer.

"Designing BoiGhor taught me the profound value of invisible UI. In a reading application, every button, border, or icon that calls attention to itself is a distraction from the story. The ultimate benchmark of success is when the interface recedes completely into the background, leaving the reader alone with the author's words. Getting out of the user's way is often the most demanding design challenge of all."

— S M Roqunuzzaman · UI/UX Designer
