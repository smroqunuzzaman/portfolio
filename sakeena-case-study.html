02 — Overview

A global platform built for structured Islamic education.

Sakeena Institute is a specialized Learning Management System (LMS) designed for international Muslim learners seeking authentic classical and contemporary education. Serving a global student base spanning the UAE, UK, US, Canada, and South Asia, the platform provides dedicated portals for three distinct user roles: students taking structured courses, scholars managing curriculum syllabi, and administrative teams managing multi-currency tuition billing.

03 — The Challenge

Generic learning software lacks cultural resonance and focused study ergonomics.

Standard Western LMS platforms (such as generic course marketplaces) are cluttered with high-friction advertising banners, poor typography for Arabic script, and rigid US-dollar-only billing. Students studying sacred texts and classical disciplines reported high cognitive distraction, an inability to cite Arabic verses easily, and checkout friction when paying from international bank cards.

✕ Generic Online Course Platforms

Cluttered Layouts & Broken Typography

Cluttered sidebars, disjointed PDF downloads, and poor multi-language support disrupt serious study.

04 — Users & Operational Roles

Three interconnected portals for a cohesive institution.

Sakeena Institute accommodates three core user roles with customized permissions and operational dashboards:

ROLE 01

International Student

Accesses enrolled courses, attends live video webinars, downloads supplementary reading PDFs, and tracks graduation milestones.

ROLE 02

Scholar / Instructor

Organizes course curriculum modules, creates chapter quizzes, manages live session schedules, and evaluates assignments.

ROLE 03

Institute Administrator

Oversees student enrollment rosters, publishes new course offerings, configures localized pricing, and issues certificates.

ROLE 04

Prospective Learner

Browses the public course catalog, reviews instructor credentials and sample syllabi, and completes streamlined registration.

07 — Key UX Decisions

Designing for reverence, cognitive calm, and global reach.

Every detail was shaped around making high-stakes sacred education comfortable for learners worldwide:

FOCUS ERGONOMICS

Distraction-Free Classroom Stage

Generic platforms crowd the video player with comments and cross-sell ads. Sakeena's classroom dims peripheral elements into a calm dark theater mode, allowing students to immerse fully in the lecture without visual clutter.

NOTE-TAKING UX

Synchronized Quranic Citations

Students frequently reference classical Arabic citations alongside lecture video. The note-taking drawer includes a quick-insert button for Arabic verse formatting, ensuring citations remain legible alongside English reflection notes.

FINANCIAL LOCALIZATION

Multi-Currency Friction Removal

International learners often abandoned course signups when forced into US dollar payments. By integrating IP-based geo-detection that automatically serves pricing in AED, GBP, EUR, or BDT, checkout anxiety was eliminated.

TIMEZONE HARMONIZATION

Global Live Session Scheduling

Because instructors teach from Dubai and students join from 100+ countries, live webinars display both the instructor's local time (GST) and the student's automatically detected local timezone with a countdown banner.

09 — Design System

A dignified aesthetic balancing modern minimalism and Islamic warmth.

The design system avoids stereotyped orientalist tropes, opting instead for contemporary editorial warmth and typographic balance:

COLOR SYSTEM

Earthy Sand, Olive Emerald & Deep Zinc

Calming sand tones (`#F4F1EA`) paired with rich olive emerald and charcoal provide high contrast without harsh glare during evening study sessions.

TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Plus Jakarta Sans & Playfair Serif

Clean sans-serif interface elements keep dashboards readable, while Playfair Display headlines infuse institutional gravitas and scholarly authority into course certificates and chapter titles.

What this project taught me as a product designer.
