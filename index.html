02 • WHY WORK WITH ME?

I design for the person using it and the team building it.

I care about understanding the problem, making thoughtful decisions, and carrying those decisions through to the details. I like clear problems, honest feedback, and work that has a real purpose.

I also use AI where it genuinely helps me explore ideas, organize information, speed up repetitive work, or test different directions. It helps me move faster, but the thinking, decisions, and responsibility for the final design are still mine.

A

I think beyond the screen.

I try to understand the product, the user, and the reason behind a feature before getting too attached to the interface.

B

I design for the real world.

Constraints, edge cases, development limitations, and changing requirements are part of the job. I try to design with them in mind.

C

AI helps me explore. I make the call.

I use AI to research, explore, iterate, and handle repetitive work. I review the output, question it, and decide what actually belongs in the product.

03 • HOW I WORK?

I don't really have a “perfect process.”

Every project starts differently. Sometimes there is a detailed brief. Sometimes there is just a problem that needs figuring out.

What stays the same is how I approach it.

01

I ask questions.

I try to understand what we are actually trying to solve before jumping into the interface.

02

I explore before I commit.

I make and test different directions instead of assuming the first idea is the right one.

03

I use whatever helps.

Figma, references, conversations, experiments, and AI tools all have a place in my process. I care more about finding the right direction than following a fixed formula.

04

I refine until it makes sense.

The goal is not to add more. It is to remove confusion and make the final experience feel clear, useful, and considered.
