02 — WHY WORK WITH ME?

I try to make complicated things feel simple.

I like understanding how a product works before deciding how it should look. I think through the user flow, the information, the edge cases, and the small interactions that make an interface feel natural.

A

I understand before I design.

I take time to understand the product, the users, and the problem before jumping into screens.

B

I think beyond individual screens.

A good interface is part of a larger journey. I design flows and systems that stay consistent as the product grows.

C

I design with implementation in mind.

I try to make my designs clear enough for developers to understand what happens, what changes, and what needs to be built.

03 — HOW I WORK

Think. Structure. Design. Refine.

I keep my process simple. I try to understand the problem first, structure the experience, design the interface, and keep refining it with feedback.

01

Understand the problem

I start with the brief, requirements, existing product, and anything else that helps me understand what we are actually solving.

02

Structure the experience

Before focusing too much on visuals, I work through user flows, information, hierarchy, and the different states of the experience.

03

Design the interface

Once the structure makes sense, I move into the visual design, components, interactions, and responsive states.

04

Review and refine

I share the work, take feedback, make adjustments, and stay close to the details until the experience feels right.
